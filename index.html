workers’ resort · closed 1999 · lower floor sealed

Someone living handed you the first fragment. Keep it somewhere you trust — it was never written down here, and it never will be.

There are three. The other two are in this place.

Everything at Niwa exists twice: once as it is, once as it remembers being. What you are reading now is only the half that agreed to be seen. The resort is quite insistent about this, and after a while you stop arguing with it.

Nothing on this page will help you. That is not the same as nothing on this page being here.

— the material reading. it is the shorter of the two.
